### 3. LinkedIn profile contact-info scrape — the *free* finder  — *2-3h*
Once we can fetch authenticated `/in/` pages, the "contact info" section sometimes exposes the verified work email (when user has set profile to public). Add this as the final-tier email source in `decision_maker.enrich` — **runs BEFORE the Reoon call**, so when it finds an email, we skip pattern-generation entirely and save the verifier credit too. This is the free-finder play, channeled through LinkedIn instead of company website.

Assign 20 mixed-segment leads (5 accountants, 5 agencies, 5 recruiters, 5 IT consultancies). Measure how many got a personal email extracted by the deeper crawl + JSON-LD. Gives a real number on Phase A uplift rather than the sample-of-2 we have today.

### 5. Decision point at end of day
Based on (4)'s number + how much the LinkedIn path unblocks, do you need Icypeas (£15/mo paid finder)? Decision goes here, not before.
