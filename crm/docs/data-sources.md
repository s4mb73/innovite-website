# UK B2B Lead-Qualification Data Sources

Brief generated by overnight research agent on 2026-04-29. Maps each
enrichment field in `crm.leads` to a recommended provider, with real
prices and integration time. Read top-to-bottom or jump to the Stack
Recommendation at the bottom.

---

## 1. Lead discovery / business directory

| Provider | What you get | Access | UK coverage | Cost | Rate limit | Integration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Places API** (Nearby + Text Search) | Name, address, phone, website, lat/lng, category | REST | Excellent | ~$32/1k Nearby calls (Advanced fields). $200/mo free credit ≈ 6k free calls | 100 QPS | Hours | Source-of-truth for "businesses physically in this city". |
| **Companies House search API** | Company name, number, address, SIC, status | REST | Excellent (UK only) | Free | 600 req / 5 min | Hours | Filter by SIC code = your TAM. No phone/email but cleanest UK firmographic seed. |
| **Apollo.io** | Firmographic + people in one shot | REST + UI | Partial-good for UK mid-market, weak on micro-SMBs | Basic $49/seat/mo (~£39), credits $0.20 each over | 600 req/min | Hours | Email accuracy 65–70%. |
| **Cognism** | Phone-verified UK contacts + firmographics | REST + UI | Excellent (their core market) | ~$15k/yr Grow + $1.5k/seat, 12-mo annual | n/a | Day+ (sales cycle) | Best UK data full-stop. Wrong tool for £200/mo/client budget. |

**Recommendation:** Seed from Companies House (free, SIC-filtered) + enrich location/contact via Google Places. Skip Apollo/Cognism for v1.

## 2. Companies House data

| Provider | What you get | Access | UK coverage | Cost | Rate limit | Integration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Companies House Public API** | Company number, SIC, incorp date, officers, filings, accounts metadata | REST | Native | Free | 600 req / 5 min | Hours | Revenue band is *not* directly exposed — you derive it from filed accounts (small-company exemption means many UK SMBs file abridged, no turnover). |

**Recommendation:** Public API for the structured fields. Add Endole only when you need turnover bands that abridged accounts hide.

## 6. LinkedIn data

This is the legal minefield. Read carefully.

| Provider | What you get | Access | UK coverage | Cost | Rate limit | Integration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Apollo.io** | LinkedIn URL, title, name (their cached copy) | REST | Partial-good | Bundled in plan | 600 req/min | Hours | **Safe** — querying *their* DB, not LinkedIn. No ToS exposure to you. |
| **PhantomBuster** | Sales Nav scrapes, profile scrapes, automation | UI + API | Universal | $59–$399/mo | Per-Phantom | Hours | Violates LinkedIn ToS. Account-ban risk on whoever's cookie. UK GDPR exposure on scraped personal data. |
| **Bright Data LinkedIn dataset** | Pre-scraped, refreshed | REST | Excellent | ~$0.001–$0.01 per record | Light | Hours | They take the legal heat, you query a dataset. Pragmatic middle ground. |
| **Cognism / Lusha** | LinkedIn-derived, licensed | REST | Cognism: excellent | Quote / £29+ for Lusha self-serve | Light | Hours | Compliance-clean. Lusha self-serve only option that ships in a day under budget. |

**Recommendation:** Apollo's cached LinkedIn URLs as default. **Don't** run PhantomBuster on a paying client's behalf — UK GDPR + LinkedIn ToS is a bad combo and you carry the controller risk.

## Stack Recommendation

If you only had budget for v1, build it with these **seven tools for ~£90–£140/mo per active client** at a few-hundred-leads/day cadence:

- **Companies House API** — free, the SIC seed
- **Google Places API** — ~£40/mo at 6k calls covered by the $200 free tier, then £20–40/mo on top
- **Apollo.io Basic** — £39/mo, one seat: covers LinkedIn URL, name, title, email
- **Anymailfinder** — pay-on-success (~£15/mo at low volume) when Apollo misses
- **Bouncer** — ~£8/mo at 1k checks for verification
- **Apify Instagram actor** — ~£3/mo at a few thousand profiles
- **PageSpeed Insights API** — free
- **Custom Python** — SSL, CTA, competitor logic — free

Roughly **£105/mo per client, all-in, fully UK-compliant, zero scraping ToS exposure, ships in a working week**.

Endole (~£29/mo) only when a client demands turnover bands abridged accounts won't reveal.

Cognism, Clay, Bright Data, PhantomBuster all stay off the v1 roadmap — bring them in only when a specific client unit-economic justifies it.
