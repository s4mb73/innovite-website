## 4. Cover image — spec (you'll need to design or commission)

**Dimensions**: 1584 × 396 px (LinkedIn cover image)

**Brief:**
- Dark background `#0b0c11` (matches website)
- One accent colour `#3d7cf5` (no purple, no gradients, no neon)
- Newsreader serif for any large text, Outfit sans for any small text
- Headline area on the left, visual on the right — text should sit in the left ⅔ so it doesn't get covered by the page logo (logo overlays the lower-left corner of the cover)
- Must be legible at the LinkedIn mobile thumbnail size (~120px wide)

**Three layout directions to brief a designer:**

**Direction A — Type only**
Big italic Newsreader serif: *"Clients find you."* on top line, *"Not the other way around."* on bottom line in accent blue. Right ⅓: simple grid of 3 anonymised metric tiles (Reply rate / Booked meetings / Pipeline growth) in the same surface treatment as the website's hero card.

**Direction B — Real content**
Behind-the-scenes still from a recent client content shoot, desaturated and tinted blue. Overlay the wordmark + tagline ("AI lead gen for B2B service firms") in the top-left.

**Direction C — Diagram**
Clean diagram showing the three pillars (AI Outbound → Content → Paid ads) feeding into one pipeline icon. Same Phosphor SVG style used in the Services section. Wordmark top-left, tagline bottom-right.

**Avoid:**
- Stock photos of "business handshakes" or "cityscapes"
- Generic AI imagery (head with circuit board, etc.)
- Gradient meshes
- More than one accent colour
